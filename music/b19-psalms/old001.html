📖 Overview｜這首歌想說什麼？

Psalmus XIII draws from Psalm 13 in the Latin Vulgate tradition. It begins with the question Usquequo, Domine?—“How long, O Lord?”—then moves through abandonment, sorrow and the plea Respice, exaudi me. Pipe organ, piano, strings, gothic choir, electric guitar and violin build a cathedral of sound around the text. When Lux appears, light becomes both the musical and visual turning point; when Ego autem in misericordia tua speravi arrives, lament begins to lean toward trust.

This is also the first song in the Bible Universe project. The dream of turning the whole Bible into music once felt too vast to touch. While experimenting with prompts, genres and languages, one simple suggestion changed the direction: what if Psalm 13 were sung in Latin? The ancient lament seemed to wake inside stone walls. It proved that Scripture did not need one uniform musical language; each passage might be waiting for a sound of its own.

Psalm 13 never pretends that faith means permanent strength. It allows grief, doubt and the frightening sense that God is silent. Yet the psalmist keeps speaking. Between Usquequo and Cantabo Domino lies a long inward journey: I do not understand the silence, but I still remember mercy; I remain in the night, but I am still willing to sing.

《Psalmus XIII》取材自拉丁《武加大譯本》的《詩篇》第十三篇。歌曲由 Usquequo, Domine?——「主啊，要到幾時？」——開始，走過被遺忘、愁苦與 Respice, exaudi me 的呼求。管風琴、鋼琴、弦樂、哥德合唱、電吉他與小提琴，共同築起一座聲音的教堂；當 Lux 出現，光成為聲音與畫面的轉折，而 Ego autem in misericordia tua speravi 則讓哀歌緩緩轉向信靠。

這也是 Bible Universe 聖經音樂計畫的第一首歌。過去，「把整本聖經寫成歌」太大、太遠，像只能放在夢裡；在練習 Prompt、曲風與語言的過程中，一句「要不要試試拉丁文？」改變了方向。古老哀歌像從石牆深處醒來，也第一次證明：聖經不必只用同一種音樂語言，每一段經文都可能正在等待自己的聲音。

《詩篇》第十三篇並未假裝信仰的人永遠堅強。它容許悲傷、疑問，也容許人覺得神正在沉默；然而詩人沒有停止向祂說話。從 Usquequo 到 Cantabo Domino，走的是一段很長的內心路：我仍不明白沉默，卻記得慈愛；我仍在黑夜，卻還願意歌唱。

📝 Lyrics & Meaning｜歌詞與意涵

🍇關鍵畫面（Key Scenes）🍇

  • Usquequo, Domine at the altar: A solitary woman kneels in a candlelit cathedral while a ghostlike choir asks, “How long?” The question is possible because faith has not disappeared.
  • Dolorem in corde meo in the corridor: Her fingers pass over cold pillars, faded frescoes and broken icons. Fragmented stained-glass light falls across a heart burning like a hidden flame.
  • Respice! Lux! breaks the darkness: Band and orchestra surge together; choirs cry from the towers as gold light pierces black clouds and turns a private prayer into a communal plea.
  • Usquequo! on the tower: The female voice cries toward the storm while a male growl answers Oblivisceris from the shadows—faith and fear inhabiting the same heart.
  • Cantabo Domino and the final beam: Stained glass shatters and gold-white light floods the sanctuary. At the end, only an empty cathedral and one pure beam remain.
  • 祭壇前的 Usquequo, Domine：女子跪在燭光教堂裡，幽靈般的合唱問「要到幾時？」正因信仰尚未消失，她才仍敢發問。
  • 長廊裡的 Dolorem in corde meo：指尖掠過石柱、壁畫與殘破聖像，碎彩光落在身上，胸口的心如暗火跳動。
  • Respice! Lux! 劈開黑暗：樂團與管弦同時湧入，高塔合唱呼喊，金光穿破黑雲，私人禱告忽然成為群體的呼求。
  • 高塔上的 Usquequo!：女聲迎向風暴吶喊，男聲從陰影低吼 Oblivisceris，讓信與懼同住於一顆心。
  • Cantabo Domino 與最後一束光：彩窗碎裂，金白光海灌入；終曲只剩空教堂與一道純白聖光。

🍇核心訊息（Core Message）🍇

Faith does not forbid questioning. “How long will You forget me?” and “I will sing to the Lord” can live in the same psalm. Grief, doubt, silence and trust may coexist; what matters is not pretending uncertainty is absent, but refusing to stop speaking to God in the deepest night.

信仰並不禁止質問。「祢忘記我要到幾時？」與「我要向主歌唱」可以存在於同一首詩裡。悲傷、疑惑、沉默與信靠能夠並存；重要的不是假裝沒有疑問，而是在最深的黑夜裡，仍不停止向神說話。

🎧 Production Notes｜編曲與製作過程

🍇情緒設計（Emotion Flow）🍇

  • Intro: Pipe organ and low strings create a vast, empty cathedral. The whispered Usquequo sounds like a question repeated through too many nights.
  • Verse 1: Piano and violin leave the female lead exposed. Long Latin vowels stretch oblivisceris, faciem and dolorem into sustained pain.
  • Pre-Chorus / Transition: There is no formal pre-chorus. Bass, drums and low frequencies gather beneath the verse until Respice feels like a long-locked door forced open.
  • Chorus: Full band, orchestra and gothic choir answer with Respice! and Lux! This is not victory yet, but a communal plea at the edge of endurance.
  • Instrumental Solo: Electric guitar and violin answer each other like two different cries, while timpani gives language a place to fall silent.
  • Verse 2: With Ego autem in misericordia tua speravi, the harmony remains dark but the rhythm becomes steadier—hope as a place to stand, not instant healing.
  • Bridge: The female Usquequo! and male growled Oblivisceris pull faith and fear against one another before the choir turns toward salvation and song.
  • Final Chorus: Miserere, Lux and Cantabo Domino overlap. Light enters without erasing the earlier darkness; the climax is trust rather than easy triumph.
  • Outro: Drums and guitars withdraw first. Organ, fading choir, haunting violin and a final whisper leave the prayer open after the music ends.
  • Intro：管風琴與低弦築起空曠教堂；耳語般的 Usquequo，像同一句質問已穿過太多夜晚。
  • Verse 1：鋼琴與小提琴讓女聲保持裸露，拉丁長音把 oblivisceris、faciem、dolorem 延展成疼痛。
  • Pre-Chorus / Transition：沒有獨立前副歌；低頻、貝斯與鼓逐步靠近，使 Respice 像終於撞開久閉的門。
  • Chorus：全樂團、管弦與哥德合唱以 Respice!、Lux! 回應。此刻不是勝利，而是走到承受極限的共同呼求。
  • Instrumental Solo：電吉他與小提琴像兩種哭聲互答，定音鼓讓語言暫時沉默。
  • Verse 2：唱到 Ego autem in misericordia tua speravi，陰影仍在，節奏卻漸穩；盼望不是立刻痊癒，而是重新站住。
  • Bridge：女聲 Usquequo! 與男聲低吼 Oblivisceris 拉扯信與懼，再由合唱帶向救恩與歌唱。
  • Final Chorus：Miserere、Lux、Cantabo Domino 交疊；光進來卻不抹去黑暗，高潮是信靠，不是廉價凱旋。
  • Outro：鼓與吉他先退去，留下管風琴、淡出合唱、小提琴與低語，讓禱告在音樂之外繼續。

🍇聲響顏色（Sound & Color）🍇

  • Instrumentation: Pipe organ defines the cathedral's vertical space; piano and strings carry fragility; timpani adds distant weight. Violin and electric guitar act as two speaking characters rather than decorative solos.
  • Vocals: The emotional female lead stays prayer-like in the verses and opens only at the climaxes. The gothic choir becomes community, memory and inner echo. Male growl appears sparingly at moments of conflict.
  • Special design: Latin is treated as an instrument. Usquequo, Respice, Lux, Misericordia and Cantabo each have a distinct shape—some made for whispers, some for choral hooks, some for a single beam of sound.
  • 樂器設定：管風琴建立教堂的垂直空間，鋼琴與弦樂承擔脆弱，定音鼓從遠處增加重量；小提琴與電吉他不是裝飾性獨奏，而是兩個彼此對話的角色。
  • 人聲設定：女聲在主歌保持禱告感，到高潮才打開音域；哥德合唱既是群體、記憶，也是內在回聲。男聲低吼只在衝突處節制出現。
  • 特別設計：把拉丁語本身當成樂器。Usquequo、Respice、Lux、Misericordia、Cantabo 各有聲響形狀：有的適合低語，有的適合群唱，有的像一道單獨的光。

🍇實作小記（Implementation Notes）🍇

Psalmus XIII began as an exploration of how far prompts, genres and languages could carry a biblical text. Could rock, orchestra, choir and pipe organ inhabit one song? Then came the simple question: What about Latin? Sung aloud, Usquequo, Domine felt less like a translation than a prayer echoing in stone for centuries. Modern rock did not push the ancient words farther away; it brought their grief closer to the body.

When the song was finished, the larger dream changed shape. If Psalm 13 could become Latin gothic symphonic rock, then another psalm could be intimate, Ecclesiastes could be lo-fi, and Habakkuk could even become punk. From here on, the question was no longer whether the Bible could become music, but: what sound is this chapter waiting for?

《Psalmus XIII》起初只是一次探索：Prompt、曲風與語言，究竟能把聖經文本帶到多遠？搖滾、管弦、合唱與管風琴能否住在同一首歌裡？後來出現一個簡單的念頭：那拉丁文呢？ 當 Usquequo, Domine 被唱出來，它不再像翻譯，而像在石牆裡迴響數百年的禱告。現代搖滾沒有讓古老文字更遙遠，反而讓那份哀傷重新靠近身體。

歌曲完成後，那個更大的夢也改變了形狀。如果《詩篇》第十三篇能成為拉丁哥德交響搖滾，另一篇詩篇可以很私密，《傳道書》可以是 Lo-fi，《哈巴谷書》甚至可以是 Punk。從此，問題不再是聖經能不能成為音樂，而是：這一章，究竟正在等待哪一種聲音？
