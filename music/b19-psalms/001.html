📖 Overview｜這首歌想說什麼？

這是詩篇系列裡的一首「時間之歌」。它不以勝利的喧聲開始，而把鏡頭放低，凝視一個疲憊的人如何在生命逐漸失去重量時，仍抬頭問神：「祢聽見嗎？」人會過去，聲音會消散，惟有神仍在——這正是它在整個系列裡所留下的位置。

取材自 《詩篇》第102篇。原詩是一位困苦者在極度疲憊中發出的禱告：年日如煙消散，心如草枯乾，自己彷彿屋頂上的孤鳥。歌詞沒有逐字搬運經文，而是將「枯乾、孤單、呼求、等候、永恆」重新整理成適合中文演唱的情緒線；旋律則建立在指定和弦 Am–Dm–G–C–F–Bm(b5)–E7–Am 上，由大提琴把哀傷沉入骨裡，再讓薩克斯風替人吹出未能說完的長嘆。

它真正追問的，也許不是「苦難為何臨到」，而是：當一切都會過去，我把生命安放在哪裡？ 人會衰老，熟悉的人會離去，掌聲與爭辯也終會散場；詩篇卻將短暫的年日放到永恆旁邊，讓一句「但祢永遠長存」成為黑夜裡的尺度。製作 MV 的那段時間，恰逢年輕的 Charlie Kirk 遇刺身亡的消息傳來，古老經文因此有了難以迴避的當代重量。這首歌不是替死亡下結論，而是獻上一句追思：願我們共同信仰的神撫慰他的家人，也願祂永遠掌權。

This is a song of time within the Psalms Series. It does not begin with the noise of victory, but lowers its gaze toward a weary soul who still asks God, “Do You hear me?” Human lives pass and voices fade, yet God remains—this is the song’s place within the series.

Inspired by Psalm 102, the prayer of a suffering soul: days vanish like smoke, the heart withers like grass, and the singer becomes a lonely bird upon a rooftop. Rather than reproducing the text word for word, the lyrics reshape its images into singable Mandarin. A fixed progression—Am–Dm–G–C–F–Bm(b5)–E7–Am—carries the journey, while cello gives grief its bodily weight and saxophone breathes out what words cannot finish.

Its deeper question is not simply “Why does suffering come?” but “If everything passes away, where do I place my life?” We age, familiar faces leave, and applause and argument eventually fall silent. Psalm 102 places our brief days beside eternity, allowing “But You endure forever” to become a measure in the night. While the MV was being made, news arrived of the death of the young Charlie Kirk. The ancient text suddenly carried an inescapable contemporary weight. This song offers no easy conclusion to death; it offers remembrance: may the God we share comfort his family, and may He reign forever.

📝 Lyrics & Meaning｜歌詞與意涵

🍇關鍵畫面（Key Scenes）🍇

主視覺以冷灰藍、風暴黑、褪色金與少量暖燭光為主色，串起孤屋、海岸、濕冷城市、空教堂與繁花燭火。薩克斯風手不是經文人物的寫實化，而是詩篇中那個無名的靈魂；最後出現的光沒有替悲傷辯解，只輕輕告訴他——你並沒有從神的眼中消失。

The main visual uses cold blue-gray, storm-black, faded gold, and occasional warm candlelight to connect a lonely house, the coast, a rain-soaked city, an empty church, and flowers lit by candles. The saxophonist embodies the psalm’s unnamed soul; the final light does not explain grief away, but whispers that he has not disappeared from the sight of God.

  • 風暴下的孤屋：金黃荒草伸向天際，屋頂只留一隻黑鳥；世界依然廣闊，人卻像被遺忘在天地中央。
    A lonely house beneath the storm: Golden fields reach the horizon, with one black bird on the roof; the world is immense, yet the soul seems forgotten at its center.
  • 海浪前的薩克斯風：樂手立在礁岩上，浪一次次碎在腳邊。那不是獻給觀眾的表演，而像一封寄不出去的信。
    Saxophone before the waves: The player stands upon the rocks as waves break below—a letter with nowhere to be sent, rather than a performance for an audience.
  • 不停步的城市：人潮從身旁經過，他抱著樂器留在原地。孤獨不是世界無人，而是自己的哀傷無人能代。
    A city that never stops: Crowds pass while he remains with his instrument. Loneliness is not an empty world, but a sorrow no one else can carry.
  • 空教堂裡的抬頭：冷藍色被窗上的光打開。他沒有立刻得到答案，只在不知道答案時，仍願意向上凝望。
    Looking up in an empty church: Light opens the cold blue interior. No instant answer comes; faith is the choice to keep looking upward.
  • 花與最後的燭火：繁花與燭光仍帶著追思，卻不再全然冰冷。有限的光裡，人繼續唱：「祢永遠長存。」
    Flowers and the final candlelight: Mourning remains, but is no longer entirely cold. Within fragile light, the soul keeps singing: “You endure forever.”

🍇核心訊息（Core Message）🍇

這首歌不是說悲傷很快就會消失，而是說：悲傷仍在時，人依然可以仰望。身體會枯乾，年日會如煙散去，思念也可能無人回答；信仰卻給生命另一個尺度——我會改變，祢不改變；我會離開，祢仍長存。盼望不是忘掉失去，而是在失去之中，仍知道自己向誰呼求。

This song does not promise that grief will quickly disappear. It says that we may still look upward while grief remains. Bodies wither, days vanish like smoke, and longing may receive no answer; faith offers another measure: I change, but You do not; I pass away, but You remain. Hope is not forgetting loss, but knowing whom to call upon within it.

🎧 Production Notes｜編曲與製作過程

🍇情緒設計（Emotion Flow）🍇

  • Intro：管風琴像黎明前尚未亮起的天色，大提琴自低處拖出陰影，男聲低吟近似呼吸，先讓「枯乾」進入空氣。
    Organ suggests a horizon before dawn; cello draws up the first shadow, and the low male hum feels closer to breathing than singing.
  • Verse：女聲近乎自言自語，鋼琴刻意留白；大提琴承接煙、火與枯草的重量，薩克斯在句尾吐出長嘆。
    The female voice is nearly private speech. Sparse piano leaves room for cello to bear the imagery, while saxophone releases a sigh at each phrase’s edge.
  • Pre-Chorus：「仰望」是第一次抬頭，小提琴與弦樂開始上行。音樂尚未爆發，只像胸口重新吸進一口氣。
    The first upward glance lifts violin and strings. It is not yet an explosion, but the first full breath after silence.
  • Chorus：私人的嘆息成為公開呼求。樂團、管弦與 gospel choir 同時展開；首遍仍克制，末遍才真正撐開天空。
    Private sorrow becomes an open plea. Band, orchestra, and gospel choir expand together; the first chorus restrains itself, while the final one opens the sky.
  • Verse 2：音色加深，從「日影偏斜」轉向「祢永遠長存」。男聲和聲進入，讓孤獨逐漸有了同行者。
    The tone darkens, then turns from declining shadows toward the God who endures. Male harmony enters, giving solitude a companion.
  • Bridge：管風琴漲起、薩克斯近乎哭喊，男女聲與合唱追問「不要掩面」。這是人在力量耗盡之前的本能祈禱。
    Organ swells and saxophone cries as voices plead, “Do not hide Your face”—an instinctive prayer at the edge of exhaustion.
  • Outro：高潮後樂器逐一退場，只留下深沉大提琴、遠去的薩克斯與耳語。悲傷未消失，卻終於找到安放之處。
    After the climax, instruments withdraw into deep cello, fading saxophone, and a whisper. Grief remains, but finally finds somewhere to rest.

🍇聲響顏色（Sound & Color）🍇

  • 樂器設定：大提琴是「身體裡的悲傷」，以低音、長音與共鳴撐住底部；薩克斯風是「說不出口的話」。管風琴建立宗教空間，鋼琴保留脆弱，小提琴與弦樂把目光往上拉。
    Cello embodies grief within the body; saxophone carries the unsaid. Organ creates sacred space, piano preserves fragility, and violin and strings lift the gaze.
  • 人聲設定：主唱採柔軟而有重量的女聲，從低處慢慢向上推；男聲只作低吟與深層回應。Gothic choir 提供陰影，gospel choir 在後段帶入共同呼求。
    A soft yet weighted female lead rises gradually, supported by low male responses. Gothic choir provides shadow; gospel choir transforms the later plea into a communal cry.
  • 特別設計：器樂承擔大量敘事，歌句之間不急著填滿，讓大提琴、薩克斯、低吟與殘響繼續說話；影像中的荒原、海岸、都市與教堂，也成為不同形態的孤獨。
    Instrumental space continues the story between lines. Cello, saxophone, humming, and reverb speak on, while wilderness, coast, city, and church become different states of solitude.

🤖 Suno Elements｜這首歌用到的 AI / Suno 設定

🍇模型與風格（Model & Style）🍇

  • Style 設計：疊合 Symphonic Rock 的厚度、Ambient Rock 的空間、Cinematic Orchestral 的敘事與聖樂合唱層次；刻意避免過度英雄化，讓高潮仍像哭過的人在唱。
    Symphonic weight, ambient space, cinematic storytelling, and sacred choral layers meet without turning the lament into heroic victory.
  • Prompt 關鍵字：symphonic rock, ambient rock, cinematic orchestral, slow tempo, emotional, atmospheric, gothic choir, gospel choir, pipe organ, strings, cello, saxophone solo, harmony, low hum chant, dramatic
  • Tempo / Key：慢速讓每個字都有停留的時間；A minor 固定和弦循環由漂泊回到歸屬，對應「年日消散，神卻長存」。
    The slow tempo lets each word remain. The fixed A-minor cycle wanders and returns, mirroring mortal days fading before the God who endures.

🍇實作小記（Implementation Notes）🍇

這首也是一次指定和弦先行的創作。先確立 Am–Dm–G–C–F–Bm(b5)–E7–Am，再思考《詩篇》102篇如何住進這組和弦；每次回到 Am，都像人在繞過自己的痛苦之後，再一次回到神面前。真正困難的不是讓音樂悲傷，而是避免它只剩悲傷：gothic choir、管風琴與管弦層若太早堆滿，容易變成末日聖樂；gospel choir 若過早進場，又會提前宣告得勝。因此前半以大提琴、鋼琴、低吟與少量薩克斯壓低聲場，直到「但祢耶和華，永遠長存」才真正轉向。

薩克斯風是這次最意外也最關鍵的一筆。它帶著人的呼吸、破碎與微微沙啞，比再添一支小提琴更能替困苦之人說話；MV 因而讓薩克斯風手穿梭於海岸、城市、教堂與孤獨房間，成為無名禱告者的視覺替身。製作期間又與 Charlie Kirk 離世的消息相遇，「我的年日如煙消散」便不再只是古老經文，而成為當代提醒：我們都以為還有明天，詩篇卻說，真正長存的從來不是我們。

This piece began with a fixed chord progression. Psalm 102 was then shaped to inhabit Am–Dm–G–C–F–Bm(b5)–E7–Am; every return to A minor feels like returning to God after circling through pain. The challenge was not making the music sad, but preventing sadness from becoming its only meaning. Gothic choir and organ could easily turn apocalyptic, while gospel choir could announce victory too soon. The first half therefore stays low and sparse until “But You, O Lord, endure forever” creates the true turn. Saxophone became the unexpected key: its breath, fracture, and slight rasp speak more humanly than another violin. During production, the news of Charlie Kirk’s death also gave “my days vanish like smoke” a contemporary weight—a reminder that we assume tomorrow is ours, while the psalm points to the One who truly endures.
