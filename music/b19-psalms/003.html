📖 Overview｜這首歌想說什麼？

How Long grows from Psalm 13 and its piercing question: “How long, O Lord? Will You forget me forever?” Rather than translating the psalm line by line, the lyrics carry its emotional truth into contemporary English poetry. “How long, O Lord, will You forget?” arrives between whisper rap, spoken word and fragile singing. Lo-fi piano carries vinyl crackle; ambient drones remain like a low frequency that never leaves the room; airy synth, reverb guitar, glitch noise and a faint violin drone appear and disappear. The arrangement does not hurry toward release because its subject is a night in which emotion seems to have nowhere to go.

Within Bible Universe, this song steps outside the assumption that sacred music must sound monumental. The same Psalm 13 had already found a voice in Latin, gothic choir and symphonic rock; here, the cathedral is removed. What remains is a prayer spoken almost against the listener’s ear. Scripture does not have only one voice, and faith does not have only one emotional register. Sometimes we cry out. Sometimes even crying out requires too much strength. On those nights, a whisper may be enough.

The visual world is a sleepless room rather than a sanctuary: a dark-haired woman by the window, city lights blurred beyond the glass, cold blue and charcoal shadows, an old record player, faded letters and one small candle. Broken stars remain the central image. They do not deny fracture; they suggest that being shattered is not the same as going dark. The final frame stands between night and dawn. Nothing is declared fixed. She is simply still here.

《How Long》取材自《詩篇》第十三篇最刺心的質問：「耶和華啊，祢忘記我要到幾時呢？」歌曲沒有逐句翻譯經文，而把它的情緒真實重新寫進現代英文詩裡。第一句介於 whisper rap、spoken word 與脆弱吟唱之間；Lo-fi 鋼琴帶著黑膠雜訊，ambient drone 像始終未離開房間的低頻，airy synth、殘響吉他、glitch noise 與若有似無的小提琴 drone 不時浮現。編曲不急著走向釋放，因為它要描寫的，正是情緒無處可去的夜晚。

在 Bible Universe 裡，這首歌離開了「聖樂必須宏大」的想像。同一篇《詩篇》13 篇，曾在拉丁語、哥德合唱與交響搖滾中找到聲音；這一次，教堂被拿掉了，只剩幾乎貼著耳朵發生的禱告。聖經不是只有一種聲音，信仰也不是只有一種情緒。有時我們呼喊；有時連呼喊都太累。那麼，低聲說也可以。

主視覺不是聖殿，而是一間沒有睡著的房間：黑髮女子坐在窗邊，城市燈火隔著玻璃失焦，冷藍與灰黑覆住四周；舊唱機、泛黃信件與一小簇燭光安靜留在桌上。「破碎的星」是核心意象，它不否認碎裂，只說明碎裂不等於熄滅。最後畫面停在夜與黎明之間，沒有宣告一切痊癒，只留下：她仍在這裡。

📝 Lyrics & Meaning｜歌詞與意涵

🍇Key Scenes｜關鍵畫面🍇

  • The sleepless window: A woman sits alone while city lights dissolve beyond the glass. She does not scream; she asks “How long, O Lord?” into cold blue silence.
  • A thousand nights: The camera moves close to tear-bright eyes where tiny lights drift like shattered stars. Sorrow has lasted long enough to become background noise.
  • The record player, letters and candle: The needle falls, old paper waits beside a frame, and memory begins to turn again. The unanswered past has not vanished; another night has pressed play.
  • Broken stars still glow: Darkness fractures into small points of light behind her. Wounded hope is not whole, but it remains alive.
  • The edge of dawn: Clouds have not fully withdrawn, yet a faint brightness rises. The image does not say “everything is fixed,” only “I am still here.”
  • 窗邊的無眠夜：女子獨坐，城市燈光在玻璃外散成模糊光點。她沒有吶喊，只向冷藍寂靜問：「主啊，要到幾時？」
  • 一千個夜晚：鏡頭貼近含淚的眼睛，微光如碎星漂浮。悲傷停留得太久，終於成了生活的背景噪音。
  • 唱機、信件與燭火：唱針落下，舊信與相框在旁等待，記憶重新旋轉。未獲回答的過去沒有消失，只是又被某個夜晚按下播放。
  • 破碎的星仍在發光：黑暗裂成細小光點，在她身後漂流。受傷的盼望並不完整，卻仍活著。
  • 黎明邊界：黑雲尚未全退，微光卻已升起。畫面不說「一切好了」，只說「我還在這裡」。

🍇Core Message｜核心訊息🍇

How Long is about faith when exhausted. Not every sorrow needs to become a scream. A person may sit in the dark and whisper, “Do You still remember me?” Before any answer arrives, continuing to call, breathe and sing can already be an act of faith. Broken stars can still shine.

《How Long》寫的是疲憊中的信仰。不是每一種悲傷都要化成吶喊。人可以坐在黑暗裡，很輕地問：「祢還記得我嗎？」在答案抵達以前，仍願意呼喊、呼吸與歌唱，本身就可能是一種信心。破碎的星，也可以發光。

🎧 Production Notes｜編曲與製作過程

🍇Emotion Flow｜情緒設計🍇

  • Intro / Whisper Rap: A glitchy lo-fi beat and ambient drone appear almost against the ear. The opening feels like a 3 a.m. confession without certainty that anyone is listening.
  • Verse 1: Lo-fi piano, vinyl crackle and soft female singing create sleepless exhaustion. Trembling details and empty space speak where a louder breakdown would feel false.
  • Pre-Chorus: There is deliberately no traditional pre-chorus. Thought drifts from verse into chorus without being told to prepare for a climax.
  • Chorus 1: Layered voices, airy synth and reverb guitar widen the room without creating a conventional explosion. “Calling” and “falling” pull against one another.
  • Verse 2: A male whisper enters over faint violin drone like another shadow in the room—not a romantic duet, but another tired soul naming the same silence.
  • Bridge: Spoken word and glitch strip language bare. “Is this weakness? No, it’s me” allows the broken self to exist without performing strength.
  • Final Chorus: The female voice opens for the first time while whisper chant and lo-fi pulse remain underneath. “Yet still I sing” lets breaking and singing coexist.
  • Outro: Ambient noise, soft choir hum and fading whispers retreat layer by layer. “Stay here, my Friend” leaves the prayer unresolved but not abandoned.
  • Intro / Whisper Rap：glitchy lo-fi beat 與 ambient drone 幾乎貼著耳朵出現，像凌晨三點的自白，不確定是否有人正在聽。
  • Verse 1：Lo-fi 鋼琴、黑膠雜訊與柔軟女聲製造無眠疲憊；細小顫抖與空白，取代不真實的大崩潰。
  • Pre-Chorus：刻意沒有傳統前副歌，思緒從主歌漂入副歌，不被安排成「現在準備高潮」。
  • Chorus 1：層疊人聲、airy synth 與殘響吉他拉寬房間，卻不形成慣常爆發；calling 與 falling 彼此拉扯。
  • Verse 2：男聲低語搭配微弱小提琴 drone，像房裡多了一道影子；不是浪漫對唱，而是另一個疲憊靈魂說出相同沉默。
  • Bridge：spoken word 與 glitch 讓語言裸露。「Is this weakness? No, it’s me」允許破碎的自己存在，不再表演堅強。
  • Final Chorus：女聲第一次真正打開，底下仍保留低語與 Lo-fi 脈搏；「yet still I sing」讓破碎與歌唱同時存在。
  • Outro：環境噪音、柔合唱與淡出低語逐層退去；「Stay here, my Friend」讓禱告沒有結論，卻也沒有被放棄。

🍇Sound & Color｜聲響顏色🍇

  • Instrumentation: Lo-fi piano keeps its grain; vinyl crackle sounds like dust left by time; ambient drone supports the piece without demanding melody. Reverb guitar stretches distance, while violin remains a faint sustained shadow rather than a decorative solo.
  • Vocals: The arrangement uses layers of distance. Female whisper is closest to the ear, soft female singing sits farther away, male whisper sounds like another room, and layered voices with soft choir hover at the edge of memory. Only the final chorus truly soars.
  • Special design: Vinyl crackle represents memory, glitch represents a tired mind breaking phrases apart, and long reverb turns the uncertainty of unanswered prayer into audible space. Imperfection is not cleaned away; it is the song’s broken beauty.
  • 樂器設定：Lo-fi 鋼琴保留顆粒，黑膠雜訊像時間的灰塵，ambient drone 托住歌曲卻不爭取旋律；殘響吉他拉遠距離，小提琴只留下持續陰影，而非裝飾性 Solo。
  • 人聲設定：不同人聲代表不同距離。女聲低語最貼近耳朵，柔女聲稍遠，男聲像另一房間，層疊人聲與 soft choir 則浮在記憶邊緣；直到最後副歌才真正高飛。
  • 特別設計：黑膠雜訊象徵記憶，glitch 象徵疲憊思緒把句子切碎，長殘響則把禱告未獲回答的距離變成可聽見的空間。不完美不被清除，它正是作品的 broken beauty。

  • Style design: Psalm 13 is placed inside a private nocturnal soundscape rather than conventional worship or symphonic sacred music. Texture matters more than virtuosity: how near, distant, fractured and empty can a prayer sound?

🍇Implementation Notes｜實作小記🍇

How Long began with a question asked after Psalm 13 had already been explored through a much larger sound: does sorrow always need to be loud? Strength is often translated into high notes, drums, guitars and a dramatic rise from despair into victory. Yet the hardest nights may contain none of that. A person can be too tired even to cry, sitting beside a window while the same question circles through the mind.

This version therefore removes the cathedral, the monumental choir and the obligation to recover on schedule. It keeps ambient drone, vinyl crackle, lo-fi piano, fragments of glitch and a whisper close to the ear. The lyrics do not translate Scripture word for word; they reshape abandonment, sleeplessness and divine silence into broken stars, smoke in rain and a name carved into scars. The smallest lines carry the greatest weight: “Yet still I breathe” and “yet still I sing.” Neither claims that suffering is over. They simply say that breathing and singing continue. Sometimes that is already faith, and sometimes worship happens at two in the morning in a room no one else knows about.

《How Long》是在《詩篇》13 篇已被做成巨大聲響之後，反過來問自己：悲傷一定要很大聲嗎？我們常把力量理解成高音、鼓、吉他，以及從絕望衝向勝利的戲劇弧線；真正難熬的夜晚卻可能什麼都沒有。人甚至累得哭不出來，只坐在窗邊，讓同一個問題不斷盤旋。

因此這個版本拿掉教堂、宏大合唱，也拿掉必須按時振作的責任，只留下 ambient drone、vinyl crackle、lo-fi piano、glitch 碎片與貼近耳邊的低語。歌詞不是逐字翻譯，而把被遺忘、失眠與神的沉默，寫成碎裂的星、雨裡的煙，以及刻在傷口上的名字。最小的句子反而最重：「Yet still I breathe」與「yet still I sing」。它們沒有宣告痛苦已經結束，只說呼吸與歌唱仍在繼續。有時這就已經是信心；有時敬拜發生在凌晨兩點，一個沒有人知道的房間裡。
